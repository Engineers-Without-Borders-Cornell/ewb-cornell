"DONDE HAY AGUA, HAY VIDA"

International SubteamTarija, Bolivia

Barrio San Cristóbal Sanitation Initiative

The 250-person community has gone without safe drinking water and sanitation since it was established over ten years ago. After a Spring 2025 assessment trip, we designed and built a public restroom and septic system there in Summer 2026. We are now redesigning the community water piping system and return in Summer 2027 to begin construction.

Implementation goal$50,000
Construction materials$30,000
Contracting & labor$15,000
Community outreach$3,000

Resume tips

01

Keep it to one page

Put your strongest, most relevant work first. Cut older or weaker items before you shrink the font or the margins.

02

Start each bullet with a verb

Say what you did, then what came of it: “Built a rain barrel system that watered 12 garden beds.” Numbers make the result concrete.

03

One line per bullet

Fill each line without spilling onto a second. Short, full lines are easier to scan than long wrapped ones.

04

Everything counts

Class projects, research, clubs, volunteering and part-time jobs all belong here. First-years can list high school; transfer students should list their previous college.

05

Name your skills

List the tools you have used, like AutoCAD, Python or Excel. Writing, design and fundraising count too: every subteam needs more than engineering.

06

Proofread, then save as PDF

Keep your contact details on one line, check every date and name, and export as a PDF so the formatting holds. A suggested file name is LastName_FirstName_Resume.pdf, though EWB does not require it.

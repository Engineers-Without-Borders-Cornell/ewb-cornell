About the Subteam

Our team is an interdisciplinary group that is responsible for raising funds for other subteams, marketing events and fundraisers on social media, and managing overall business operations for our chapter.

Over the past several years, our team has successfully run crowdfunding and Giving Day campaigns (Access to Water: Irrigation for Tanzania and Sustainable Housing Units (SHUs) - Portable Homes for All) to raise over $60,000 for both the design and implementation of a water irrigation system in Sunuka, Tanzania and a portable home to assist members in need from our local community. We continue to brainstorm and carry out creative ways to engage alumni and the wider public to donate and learn more about our projects.

In the past, we also designed a text-to-image processor to streamline communication for the Tanzania subteam's irrigation system installation. In using natural language processing and machine learning techniques, the team executed the design of a rule and constraint based algorithm which inputs instructional text and outputs corresponding image sequences.

If you are interested in making a donation or becoming a corporate sponsor, please reach out via our email below.

About the Subteam

The software team builds mobile applications for local sustainability partners. We are currently building a reuse marketplace with Finger Lakes ReUse. We have also completed a professional-clothing rental platform with Cornell Wardrobe and a carbon-offset app with the Tompkins Climate Fund.

Our Work

Projects

Active Software

Finger Lakes ReUse App

A mobile marketplace that encourages the community to donate, reuse, and purchase pre-owned goods, designed to be simple for users of all ages and abilities. The core app and database are functional as we work toward a complete prototype and handoff.

Ithaca, NY React Native/Node.js Active
Completed Software

EWB x Cornell Wardrobe App

A mobile platform that gives students a convenient way to browse and rent professional clothing from their phones, built with Swift and Firebase and paired with an internal inventory system for Cornell Wardrobe.

Cornell Campus Swift/Firebase Completed
Completed Software

Climate Fund App

A mobile app that connects individual climate action with local environmental initiatives, calculating a user’s carbon footprint and directing offset contributions to the Finger Lakes Climate Fund.

Tompkins County, NY React Native/Expo Go Completed

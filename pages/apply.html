Do engineering that matters.

Engineers and non-engineers from all 16 fields of study are welcome. No minimum GPA, just a commitment to service. Applications open each semester.

01 / How It Works

Four steps to joining.

STEP 01

Come to an info session

Drop by an info session to meet members and hear about the current projects. No commitment.

STEP 02

Submit the form

Tell us your interests and which subteams appeal to you. The application is short.

STEP 03

Casual conversation

Selected applicants are invited for a brief, informal chat, not a stressful interview.

STEP 04

Join a subteam

Get matched to a team and start contributing to a real project right away.

02 / Interest Form

Tell us where you want to build.

This sends an interest signal. We will follow up by email with the official application when applications open.

Prefer email? Reach us at ewb@cornell.edu.

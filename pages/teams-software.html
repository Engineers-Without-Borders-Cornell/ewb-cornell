Our Work

Projects

Closed Software

EWB x Cornell Wardrobe App

In the Spring 2023 Semester, our team and Cornell Wardrobe collaborated to create a mobile application that allows students to rent professional attire directly from their phones. Developed using Swift and Firebase, we aim to release an MVP (Minimum Viable Product) later this year. Additionally, a new inventory management system has been implemented for Cornell Wardrobe using Monday, Make, and Firebase, enabling real-time updates on clothing availability.

Cornell Campus Swift/Firebase Closed
Closed Software

Climate Fund App

Our team has been working on an app in collaboration with the Tompkins Climate Fund, allowing you to carbon offset, a process by which you donate money in order to account for your carbon footprint. All donations go to the Finger Lakes Climate Fund, which then uses the money to invest in local sustainable projects

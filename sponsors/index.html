02 / What Your Gift Funds

Unit costs by project.

These are the line items a contribution covers across our three project areas.

International / Bolivia
$100 / meter
Sewage & sanitation
  • $100 funds 1 meter of sanitary sewage piping, connecting a household to safe sanitation.
  • $5,000 maintains utility networks: yearly repairs that keep families healthy.
  • $10,000 builds a full septic tank unit for 20+ households.
Fund Bolivia
Domestic / Austin
$150 / panel
Water & irrigation
  • $150 covers 1 solar panel powering irrigation with renewable energy.
  • $750 funds a rainwater harvesting cistern design for drought-prone areas.
  • $2,500 supplies a drip irrigation system for the community garden.
Fund Austin
Software & Digital Ag
$100 / license
Open-source tools
  • $100 sponsors a server or data hosting license, keeping our tools free.
  • $500 funds open-source development tools and web services.
  • $1,000 covers drone and rover system materials for crop detection.
